# Authentication and Account Management — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI accounts provide secure access to your family's infant health data, cry analyses, activity timelines, and community resources. This guide walks you through signing up, completing onboarding, signing in, resetting your password, and managing your account settings.

---

## Creating an Account (Sign Up)

Signing up for MumtaAI is quick and free.

### Step-by-Step Sign-Up Instructions:
1. Navigate to the **Sign Up** page.
2. Enter your details:
   - **Full Name**: Your preferred display name.
   - **Email Address**: Use an active email where you can receive verification links.
   - **Password**: Create a secure password (must be at least 6 characters long).
3. Click **Create Account**.
4. Check your email inbox for a verification email from MumtaAI.
5. Click the **Verify Email** link in the email to activate your account.

---

## Role Intent Selection

After verifying your email, MumtaAI asks you to declare your role:
- **"I'm a Parent / Family Member"**: Select this if you are a mother, father, guardian, or relative caring for an infant. You will immediately proceed to the Baby Onboarding Wizard.
- **"I'm a Healthcare Professional"**: Select this if you are a certified pediatrician, lactation consultant, nurse, or sleep expert wishing to publish articles and assist parents. You will be directed to submit your professional credentials for verification.

---

## New Parent Onboarding Wizard

When you first join as a parent, MumtaAI guides you through registering your baby:

1. **Baby Information**:
   - Baby's Name
   - Date of Birth
   - Gender (Boy, Girl, or Prefer not to say)
   - Birth Measurements: Weight (in kg) and Height (in cm)
2. **Optional Baby Photo**: Upload a cute photo of your baby for their profile card.
3. **Finish Setup**: Click **Complete Onboarding**. Your personalized dashboard is immediately generated.

---

## Signing In

1. Go to the **Sign In** page.
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. You will be directed directly to your active baby's dashboard.

---

## How to Reset a Forgotten Password

If you forget your password, you can reset it securely at any time:

1. On the Sign In screen, click **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Click **Send Reset Link**.
4. Check your email inbox for the password reset email.
5. Click the link provided in the email.
6. Enter and confirm your new password.
7. Click **Save New Password**.
8. Sign in with your new password.

> [!NOTE]
> Password reset links are time-sensitive for your protection. If the link expires, simply request a new one from the Forgot Password screen.

---

## Managing Your Profile & Account Details

You can view and update your personal account information at any time:

1. Open your profile menu in the upper-right corner and select **Settings**.
2. Under the **Profile** tab, you can update:
   - **Full Name**: Change your display name shown to caregivers and community members.
   - **Profile Picture**: Upload or change your personal avatar.
   - **Phone Number**: Add an optional contact number.
   - **Timezone**: Set your local timezone to ensure accurate timestamps on activity logs and vitals charts.
   - **Language Preference**: Choose your preferred display language.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## Signing Out

To securely sign out of your account:
1. Click your profile avatar or menu in the top navigation bar.
2. Select **Sign Out**.
3. You will be safely signed out across all active tabs and returned to the welcome screen.

---

## Common Login and Account Issues & Troubleshooting

### 1. "I didn't receive my verification or password reset email"
- Check your **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder.
- Ensure you typed your email address correctly with no extra spaces.
- Wait 2–3 minutes for the email server to deliver the message.
- If you still haven't received it, return to the sign-in screen and click **Resend verification email** or request a new reset link.

### 2. "Invalid email or password" error
- Ensure your Caps Lock key is off.
- Double-check for typing errors in your email address.
- If you cannot remember your password, use the **Forgot Password?** link to set a new one.

### 3. "Account Suspended" notice
- If your account displays a suspended message upon logging in, your account may have been locked due to a suspected terms-of-service violation or payment dispute. Please contact MumtaAI Support via the contact form to appeal or resolve the suspension.

### 4. "Session Expired" or unexpected logout
- For security purposes, if your account status changes (such as a password change on another device or security update), MumtaAI will refresh your session or ask you to log in again to safeguard your baby's data.
