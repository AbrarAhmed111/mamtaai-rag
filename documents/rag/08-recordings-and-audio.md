# Audio Recordings and Management — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI allows parents and caregivers to record infant crying live or upload audio files to run through AI cry analysis. Every recording is securely stored and associated with your baby's profile, allowing you to review past audio clips and see how crying patterns change over time.

---

## How to Record Infant Cry Audio

MumtaAI offers two easy methods to provide audio for analysis:

### 1. Live Microphone Recording (Recommended)
You can record your baby's crying directly through your web browser on your phone, tablet, or laptop:

1. Open your MumtaAI dashboard and click **Analyze Cry** (or the microphone icon).
2. Ensure your baby is selected.
3. Bring your device reasonably close to your baby (ideally 1 to 2 meters / 3 to 6 feet away).
4. Tap **Start Recording**.
5. Let the app record about **8 seconds** of clear infant crying.
6. Tap **Stop & Analyze** (or let the timer complete automatically).
7. The audio is immediately processed, and your cry analysis results appear on screen within seconds!

### 2. Uploading an Audio File
If you captured a clip using your smartphone's voice memo app or downloaded an audio file from your smart baby monitor:
1. Navigate to **Analyze Cry** and select the **Upload Audio** tab.
2. Click **Browse Files** or drag and drop your audio clip.
3. Supported audio formats: **MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG, and WebM**.
4. Click **Analyze Uploaded Audio**.

## Best Practices for Accurate Cry Recording

To ensure the AI classifier produces the most accurate insights:
- **Minimize Background Noise**: Try to turn down television audio, loud fans, or background music.
- **Keep Moderate Distance**: Hold the microphone 1 to 2 meters from the infant. Placing it too close can distort the sound, while placing it too far might capture too much room echo.
- **Capture Active Crying**: Make sure the clip captures uninterrupted crying rather than quiet whimpers, hiccups, or caregiver talking.
- **Aim for 8 to 15 Seconds**: An 8-second continuous cry clip provides optimal acoustic data for our classification model.

## Reviewing Past Recordings

You can listen back to previous cry recordings and review the classification results:
1. Navigate to **Cry History** or **Recordings** in your dashboard.
2. Browse recordings sorted by date and time.
3. Click the **Play** button on any audio card to listen to the clip.
4. Click **View Details** to review the predicted distress cause, confidence percentage, urgency score, and soothing advice given at that time.

## Privacy and Audio Security

Your family's privacy is our top priority:
- **Strictly Private**: Infant audio clips are stored in private, encrypted cloud storage.
- **No Public Access**: Only the Primary Parent and authorized caregivers can play back your baby's recordings.
- **Deleting Audio**: Primary parents can permanently delete any audio recording from the history view at any time.

## Troubleshooting Audio and Microphone Issues

### 1. The browser says "Microphone Permission Denied"
- When you first tap record, your browser will prompt: *"Allow MumtaAI to access your microphone?"*
- If you accidentally tapped "Block" or "Deny":
  - **On Google Chrome (Mobile/Desktop)**: Tap the padlock or settings icon in the browser address bar, go to **Permissions > Microphone**, and change it to **Allow**.
  - **On Safari (iOS / Mac)**: Go to your device **Settings > Safari > Microphone** and select **Allow**.
  - Refresh the page and try recording again.

### 2. "Audio was too quiet or unclear"
- Move the phone slightly closer to your baby and record during continuous crying.
- Check that your phone's microphone is not physically covered by your hand or a thick case.

### 3. "Monthly Cry Analysis Limit Reached"
- If you are on the Free plan and reach your 7 monthly analyses, you can upgrade to the **Plus Plan** for 60 analyses per month or the **Pro Plan** for unlimited analyses.
