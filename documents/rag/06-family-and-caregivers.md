# Family and Caregivers — MumtaAI

## What are Caregivers in MumtaAI?

Raising a child is a team effort. MumtaAI allows the **Primary Parent** to invite partners, grandparents, nannies, and babysitters as **Caregivers** to share access to a baby's profile.

Instead of asking "When was the last feeding?" or "How long was the nap?", everyone involved in your baby's daily routine can view and log updates in real time on one unified platform.

---

## Plan Limits on Caregivers

Family sharing is available on the Plus and Pro plans:

| Plan | Caregivers Allowed |
|---|:---:|
| **Free Plan** | **0 caregivers** (Family sharing not included) |
| **Plus Plan** ($9.99/mo) | **Up to 2 caregivers per baby** |
| **Pro Plan** ($19.99/mo) | **Unlimited caregivers per baby** |

If you are currently on the Free plan, upgrading to Plus or Pro instantly unlocks the ability to invite family members and caregivers.

## Caregiver Access Levels Explained

When inviting a caregiver, you choose their permission level based on how much control they need:

### 1. Full Access
- **Best for**: Co-parents, spouses, partners, or full-time nannies.
- **Permissions**:
  - Can log, edit, and delete all 8 activity types.
  - Can record audio for cry analysis and view prediction results.
  - Can connect a Bluetooth pulse oximeter and start monitoring sessions.
  - Can view complete activity history and analytical health insights.
  - Can update medical notes and pediatrician contact info.
  - Can export pediatrician summary reports (if plan allows).
- **Restrictions**: Cannot invite new caregivers, change permissions, modify vital alert thresholds, or delete the baby profile.

### 2. Read-Only Access
- **Best for**: Grandparents, family doctors, or relatives who want to stay informed without editing records.
- **Permissions**:
  - Can view live pulse oximeter readings and historical vitals charts.
  - Can browse past feeding, sleep, and diaper logs.
  - Can view milestone achievements.
- **Restrictions**: Cannot log or delete activities, cannot record cry audio, and cannot pair devices.

### 3. Limited Access
- **Best for**: Babysitters, daycare staff, or casual helpers.
- **Permissions**:
  - Can log routine daily care activities (specifically bottle feedings and diaper changes).
- **Restrictions**: Cannot view historical cry analyses or deep health trends, cannot connect devices, and cannot edit profile settings.

## How to Invite a Caregiver

1. Log in as the **Primary Parent** and select your baby.
2. From the main dashboard menu, click **Family & Caregivers** (or **Invite Caregiver**).
3. Enter the caregiver's details:
   - **Email Address**: The email where the invitation should be sent.
   - **Relationship**: Select their relation to the baby (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent, Nanny, Babysitter, Other).
   - **Access Level**: Choose **Full Access**, **Read-Only**, or **Limited**.
4. Click **Send Invitation**.
5. An invitation link is emailed to the caregiver, and an active invite link is also displayed on your screen for you to copy and share directly (e.g., via SMS or WhatsApp).

---

## How Caregivers Accept an Invitation

1. The caregiver opens the email or clicks the invitation link shared with them.
2. If they do not already have a MumtaAI account, they sign up for free (they do not need to purchase a paid subscription to join your baby).
3. If they already have an account, they simply sign in.
4. They will see an on-screen prompt: *"Accept invitation to join [Baby's Name] as a caregiver?"*
5. They click **Accept Invitation**.
6. The baby's profile immediately appears in their dashboard and baby selector dropdown!

---

## How to Change a Caregiver's Access Level

You can change a caregiver's permission level at any time:
1. Navigate to **Family & Caregivers**.
2. Find the caregiver in your list.
3. Click the access level dropdown next to their name and select the new level (Full, Read-Only, or Limited).
4. Click **Update**. The changes apply immediately.

---

## How to Remove a Caregiver

If a caregiver no longer needs access:
1. Go to **Family & Caregivers**.
2. Locate the caregiver and click **Remove** (or the trash icon).
3. Confirm the removal prompt.
4. Their access is revoked instantly. The baby profile will immediately vanish from their dashboard.

## Troubleshooting Caregiver Invitations

### 1. The caregiver didn't receive the invitation email
- Ask the caregiver to check their **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder.
- You can copy the direct invitation link from your **Family & Caregivers** page and send it to them directly via text message or chat.

### 2. "Invitation link has expired or is invalid"
- Invitation links expire after a designated period for security reasons.
- In your **Family & Caregivers** page, cancel the pending invitation and send a fresh one.

### 3. "Maximum caregiver limit reached"
- On the **Plus Plan**, you can invite up to 2 caregivers per baby.
- If you need to invite more family members or helpers, upgrade to the **Pro Plan** for unlimited caregivers.
