# Notifications System — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI's notification system ensures you stay informed about your baby's vital signs, cry analysis results, daily routine updates from caregivers, and community answers without overwhelming you with noise.

---

## Types of Notifications

### 1. Pulse Oximeter Safety Alerts (Urgent Priority)
- Sent when your baby's oxygen saturation (SpO2) or pulse rate stays outside your customized safe limits for more than 5 continuous seconds.
- Triggers an instant on-screen alert banner and an audible chime on your device.

### 2. High-Urgency Cry Alerts
- Surfaces when an infant cry is classified with high or critical situational distress (such as suspected pain or colic), prompting immediate parental comfort checks.

### 3. Caregiver & Family Activity Updates
- Notifies you when an invited partner, grandparent, or nanny logs a bottle feeding, administers medication, or records a milestone.
- Notifies invited caregivers when they receive an invitation to join a baby profile.

### 4. Milestone Celebrations & Daily Reminders
- Congratulates you when developmental milestones are recorded.
- Provides helpful reminders if you have scheduled vitamin drops or bedtime routines.

### 5. Community Forum & Expert Responses
- Alerts you when a verified healthcare expert or fellow parent answers your question in the community forums.

### 6. Subscription & Account Notices
- Alerts you when your monthly cry analysis or activity limits are approaching.
- Informs you of billing renewals, invoice receipts, or changes to your subscription plan.

---

## Where Notifications Appear

1. **The Notification Bell**: Located in the top navigation header. A red dot badge displays your unread notification count.
2. **Notification Drawer**: Clicking the bell opens your notification history where you can read, click to navigate, or click **Mark All as Read**.
3. **In-App Visual Banners (Toasts)**: Timely alerts appear briefly in the top corner of your screen while you are actively using MumtaAI.
4. **Email Notifications**: Important account receipts, caregiver invitations, and security notices are sent directly to your registered email address.

---

## Customizing Your Notification Preferences

You have full control over what alerts you receive and when:

1. Open the profile menu and navigate to **Settings > Notifications**.
2. **Toggle Notification Categories**:
   - Enable or disable Cry Alerts, Milestone Reminders, Community Replies, or Marketing Updates.
   - Note: Critical vital sign safety alerts during active oximeter sessions remain enabled by default for baby safety.
3. **Configure Quiet Hours**:
   - Set a bedtime window (e.g., 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM) during which routine notifications (such as community replies or non-urgent tips) are silenced.
   - Check **Allow Urgent Alerts during Quiet Hours** so you are still immediately alerted if an active oximeter session registers a sustained threshold breach.
4. Click **Save Notification Preferences**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why didn't I hear an audible chime when an oximeter alert fired?
- Check your device's physical volume or silent/vibrate switch (on laptops and mobile devices, sound may be muted by system volume settings).
- Make sure your browser tab has not been placed into sleeping mode by your operating system.
- Check that your browser has permission to play audio.

### How do I clear unread notifications?
Open the notification bell drawer in the top navigation bar and click **Mark All as Read** at the top of the panel.
