# Cry Analysis — MumtaAI

## How Does Cry Analysis Work?

Babies cry to communicate basic needs, but for tired or anxious parents, telling cries apart can be overwhelming. MumtaAI uses advanced acoustic analysis trained on thousands of infant vocalizations to help decode the subtle sound patterns in your baby's cry.

When you record or upload an 8-second audio clip of your crying baby:
1. **Background Noise Filtering**: The system filters out room ambient noise (fans, air conditioning, distant talking).
2. **Acoustic Pattern Analysis**: Key acoustic features such as pitch, rhythm, harmonics, and pauses are analyzed.
3. **Smart Classification**: The cry is matched against known acoustic distress patterns.
4. **Instant Actionable Guidance**: You immediately receive the predicted primary cause of distress, a confidence rating, an urgency score, and step-by-step soothing recommendations.

## Recognized Cry Categories

MumtaAI recognizes 7 primary infant distress categories:

| Cry Category | Common Sound Characteristics | What It Typically Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Hunger (`hungry`)** | Rhythmic, repetitive cry that escalates in volume and pitch. Often accompanied by lip smacking or root reflex. | Baby is due for a feeding (breast or bottle). |
| **Sleepy / Tired (`tired`)** | Whiny, nasal cry, often accompanied by yawning, eye rubbing, or looking away. | Baby is overtired or ready for a nap/bedtime routine. |
| **Discomfort (`discomfort`)** | Whimpering or fussing sound; variable pitch. Often linked to room temperature, scratchy clothing, or awkward position. | Baby needs repositioning, cooler/warmer clothes, or physical comforting. |
| **Pain (`pain`)** | Sudden onset, high-pitched, intense screech or scream, followed by a brief breath-holding pause. | Baby is experiencing acute physical discomfort, teething pain, or stomach cramps. |
| **Diaper Change (`diaper_change`)** | Restless fussing and writhing, often with grunting sounds. | Baby has a wet or soiled diaper that feels cold or irritated against their skin. |
| **Colic (`colic`)** | Prolonged, intense bouts of inconsolable crying, often with a tense belly, clenched fists, and drawn-up legs. | Gastrointestinal gas, immature digestion, or infant colic. |
| **Overstimulated (`overstimulated`)** | Fussy, jerky crying, turning head away from lights, sounds, or faces. | Baby has experienced too much sensory input and needs a quiet, dimly lit room. |

## Understanding Your Cry Analysis Results

When analysis finishes, your results screen displays:

### 1. Primary Predicted Cause
The most probable reason for the cry (e.g., *"Hunger - 84% Match"*).

### 2. Confidence Rating
Shows how strongly the acoustic patterns aligned with that category. A high confidence rating (75%+) indicates clear, textbook acoustic markers. Lower confidence ratings may occur if there was significant background noise or if your baby has overlapping needs (e.g., both tired and hungry).

### 3. The Urgency Meter
The Urgency Meter provides a clear, calm situational indicator:
- **Low (Green)**: Routine baby need. Fits standard home care checks (feed, check diaper, or cuddle).
- **Moderate / Medium (Yellow)**: Baby needs attention soon. Step through feeding, nap, or comfort routines over the next few minutes.
- **High (Orange)**: Pressing discomfort. Comfort your baby closely; check for tight clothing, fever, or gas pain.
- **Critical (Red)**: Severe distress. If your baby is completely inconsolable, in pain, feverish, or lethargic, seek medical advice promptly.

### 4. Actionable Soothing Tips
Specific non-medical recommendations tailored to the predicted distress type (e.g., burping techniques for colic, swaddling and white noise for tiredness, or pacing feeds for hunger).

### 5. Medical Red Flags
If the acoustic intensity or accompanying signs point to potential illness, MumtaAI highlights common pediatric warning signs (such as high fever, difficulty breathing, or continuous inconsolability) with instructions on when to consult your pediatrician.

## Troubleshooting Cry Analysis

### 1. Analysis says "Low Confidence" or "Uncertain"
- Ensure the recording captures at least 8 seconds of continuous crying.
- Move the microphone closer to your baby (around 1–2 meters).
- Reduce background noise such as televisions, loud music, or nearby conversations.

### 2. "Monthly Analysis Limit Reached"
- Free plan accounts receive 7 analyses per month.
- Upgrade to **Plus** (60/month) or **Pro** (Unlimited) to continue analyzing cries without interruption.
