# Troubleshooting and Limitations — MumtaAI

## Overview

If you encounter an issue, an error notice, or an unexpected limit in MumtaAI, this guide explains what the message means and the exact steps to resolve it quickly.

---

## 1. Subscription Plan Limit Messages

When you reach the monthly allowance for your current subscription plan, MumtaAI displays a friendly notification explaining the limit. Your saved data is never lost or deleted when you hit a limit.

| Notice / Message | What Happened | How to Resolve It |
|---|---|---|
| **"Monthly Cry Analysis Limit Reached"** | You have used all monthly cry analyses included in your plan (Free plan: 7/month; Plus plan: 60/month). | Upgrade to **Plus** (60 analyses) or **Pro** (unlimited), or wait for your monthly billing cycle to reset. |
| **"Baby Profile Limit Reached"** | You attempted to add more babies than allowed (Free plan: 1 baby; Plus plan: 3 babies). | Upgrade to **Plus** (up to 3 babies) or **Pro** (unlimited babies) from **Settings > Subscription**. |
| **"Caregiver Invites Disabled"** | You attempted to invite a family member while on the Free plan. | Upgrade to **Plus** (up to 2 caregivers/baby) or **Pro** (unlimited caregivers) to share baby profiles. |
| **"Maximum Caregivers Reached"** | You have already invited 2 caregivers on the Plus plan. | Upgrade to **Pro** for unlimited caregiver invitations per baby. |
| **"Activity Limit Reached"** | You reached the 20 monthly activity logs on the Free plan. | Upgrade to **Plus** or **Pro** for unlimited daily care tracking across all 8 activity categories. |
| **"Report Export Not Included"** | You attempted to download a pediatrician PDF/CSV report on the Free plan. | Upgrade to **Plus** (1 export/month) or **Pro** (unlimited exports) to download health summaries. |

## 2. Bluetooth Pulse Oximeter Troubleshooting

| Issue / Error | Likely Cause | Step-by-Step Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **"Web Bluetooth is not supported on this browser"** | You are using an unsupported browser (such as Safari or Chrome on an iPhone or iPad, or Firefox). | Open MumtaAI using **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on an Android phone, Windows PC, or Mac. Direct Bluetooth pairing is not supported on iOS due to Apple platform restrictions. |
| **"Device not found" or pairing popup is empty** | The oximeter is turned off, has a low battery, or is out of range. | 1. Turn the pulse oximeter on and place it within 2 meters (6 feet) of your computer or phone.<br>2. Ensure Bluetooth is switched ON in your device system settings.<br>3. If on Android, ensure **Location** is turned ON (required by Android for Bluetooth scanning). |
| **"Connection timed out or failed to connect"** | The oximeter is currently connected to another phone, tablet, or companion app. | Turn off Bluetooth on nearby devices or close other oximeter apps, then tap **Connect Oximeter** again. |
| **"Readings Stale" or vitals stop updating** | The sensor may have slipped off your baby's foot or toe, or your baby moved out of range. | 1. Gently check sensor placement on your baby's foot or hand.<br>2. Keep your device in the same room.<br>3. MumtaAI will automatically attempt to reconnect within a few seconds. |

## 3. Cry Analysis and Microphone Troubleshooting

| Issue / Error | Likely Cause | Step-by-Step Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **"Microphone Permission Denied"** | The browser was blocked from accessing your microphone. | 1. Tap the lock or tune icon in your browser address bar.<br>2. Find **Microphone** and switch it to **Allow**.<br>3. Refresh the page and tap **Start Recording** again. |
| **"No audio detected" or "Audio too quiet"** | Microphone was muted, covered by a hand or case, or held too far away. | Bring the phone or microphone closer to your baby (1 to 2 meters) and record during active crying. |
| **"Unsupported Audio Format"** | The uploaded file is in an unrecognized or corrupted format. | Ensure your uploaded file is in standard **MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG, or WebM** format. |
| **"Cry Analysis Temporarily Unavailable"** | Temporary service interruption during routine maintenance. | Please wait 1–2 minutes and try again, or check your internet connection. |

## 4. Account and Login Issues

| Issue / Error | Likely Cause | Step-by-Step Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **"Invalid email or password"** | Typo in credentials or Caps Lock is ON. | Check your spelling and Caps Lock, or click **Forgot Password?** to reset your password. |
| **"Please verify your email"** | Account was created but the verification link was not clicked. | Check your email inbox and spam folder for the verification email and click the link. |
| **"Account Suspended"** | Account was flagged for policy violation or billing dispute. | Contact **support@mumtaai.com** to review your account status. |

## 5. Important Safety Disclaimers & System Boundaries

> [!CAUTION]
> **What MumtaAI Does NOT Do**
> 
> 1. **No Emergency Services Dispatch**: MumtaAI does **not** call 911, contact local emergency dispatchers, or alert hospitals when vital signs breach thresholds. Parents must initiate emergency calls.
> 2. **Not a Medical Diagnostic Device**: Cry predictions and vitals monitoring are informational parenting tools. They do not diagnose diseases, respiratory distress, heart conditions, or physical trauma.
> 3. **Environmental Noise Sensitivity**: Cry classification accuracy decreases in loud environments (loud televisions, radios, or talking). Always record in a reasonably quiet room for best results.
