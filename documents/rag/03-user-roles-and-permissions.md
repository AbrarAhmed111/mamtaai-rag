# User Roles and Permissions — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI uses a clear, role-based access system to protect your family's sensitive health data while making it easy to collaborate. Different users have distinct capabilities depending on whether they are the baby's primary parent, an invited caregiver, or a verified healthcare professional.

---

## The Three User Roles

### 1. Primary Parent
The **Primary Parent** is the account owner who creates the baby's profile in MumtaAI.

**What a Primary Parent can do:**
- Create and edit baby profile details (name, birth date, measurements, medical notes, photos).
- Invite caregivers (spouses, partners, grandparents, nannies) and assign their access level.
- Change or revoke caregiver permissions at any time.
- Set custom pulse oximeter vital alert thresholds for their baby.
- Record infant audio for cry analysis and validate predictions.
- Connect Bluetooth pulse oximeters and save monitoring sessions.
- Log, edit, and delete all 8 daily care activity types.
- View all historical health trends and export pediatrician reports (on Plus and Pro plans).
- Permanently remove or deactivate baby profiles.

**Limitations:**
- Must operate within active subscription tier quotas (e.g., maximum babies, monthly cry analysis limits).

---

### 2. Invited Caregiver
A **Caregiver** is a trusted individual (e.g., partner, co-parent, grandparent, babysitter, or nanny) who has accepted an email or link invitation to collaborate on a baby's profile.

Caregivers do **not** have their own separate baby profiles for your child; instead, they access your shared baby profile with one of three access levels assigned by the Primary Parent:

#### A. Full Access
Best for co-parents, partners, or full-time nannies who actively manage day-to-day care.
- **Can**:
  - Log, edit, and delete daily activities (feeding, sleep, diapers, medicine, etc.).
  - Record infant cry audio and view cry analysis results.
  - Connect a Bluetooth pulse oximeter and start monitoring sessions.
  - View full activity histories, trends, and analytical insights.
  - Update medical care notes.
  - Export pediatrician summary reports (if the primary account is on Plus/Pro).
- **Cannot**:
  - Invite new caregivers or remove existing ones.
  - Change other caregivers' permission levels.
  - Modify oximeter alert thresholds.
  - Delete or deactivate the baby profile.

#### B. Read-Only Access
Best for grandparents, extended relatives, or family members who want to follow along without logging entries.
- **Can**:
  - View live oximeter readings and historical vitals charts.
  - Browse past activity timeline logs and cry analysis results.
  - View baby growth stats and milestones.
- **Cannot**:
  - Log or delete activities.
  - Record audio for cry analysis.
  - Pair or start new oximeter monitoring sessions.
  - Edit baby profile details or invite caregivers.

#### C. Limited Access
Best for casual babysitters, daycare providers, or helpers who only need to record basic daily routines.
- **Can**:
  - Log routine daily care activities (such as bottle feedings and diaper changes).
- **Cannot**:
  - View past cry analysis predictions or detailed historical health trends.
  - Pair oximeters or modify baby profile information.
  - Export health reports or manage family members.

---

### 3. Healthcare Expert
A **Healthcare Expert** is a certified medical professional (such as a pediatrician, pediatric nurse, lactation consultant, or infant sleep coach) whose professional credentials have been formally verified by the MumtaAI team.

**What Healthcare Experts do on MumtaAI:**
- Publish authoritative, evidence-based articles in the MumtaAI Community Blog.
- Answer questions and provide general parenting guidance in the Community Forums.
- Display a "Verified Expert" badge next to their profile name.
- Switch between their **Expert Workspace** (for community authoring) and standard **Parent Workspace** (for tracking their own family).

**Important Boundaries:**
- Healthcare experts provide educational guidance and general parenting tips. They **cannot** write medical prescriptions or diagnose medical conditions through MumtaAI.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a caregiver delete my baby's profile?
No. Only the Primary Parent who originally created the baby profile has the authority to delete or deactivate it.

### Can an invited caregiver invite other people?
No. Only the Primary Parent has permission to generate invitation links or add new caregivers to a baby's profile.

### How do I change a caregiver's permission level?
As the Primary Parent, go to **Family & Caregivers** in your dashboard, locate the caregiver's name, select their current access level, and choose a new level (Full, Read-Only, or Limited) from the dropdown.

### What happens if I revoke a caregiver's access?
The caregiver immediately loses access to your baby's profile, logs, and vitals. If they log in, your baby will no longer appear in their dashboard.

### Can a healthcare expert see my baby's personal health data?
No. Healthcare experts only have access to public community discussions and articles they author. They cannot view your private baby profiles, cry recordings, or oximeter data unless you explicitly invite them as a personal caregiver.
