# Security and Privacy — MumtaAI

## Our Commitment to Your Family's Privacy

At MumtaAI, we understand that infant health records, daily feeding logs, personal photos, and audio recordings are deeply personal. We treat your family's data with the highest standard of privacy, confidentiality, and security.

This guide explains how your data is protected, who can access it, how payment security works, and your rights to manage or delete your data at any time.

---

## What Data MumtaAI Collects and Why

We only collect information necessary to provide you with smart childcare tracking and insights:

1. **Account Details**: Your name, email address, and optional phone number to manage your login, password recovery, and subscription receipts.
2. **Baby Profile Data**: Your baby's name, date of birth, gender, birth measurements (weight/height), and medical care notes.
3. **Daily Care Logs**: Timestamps and details of feedings, sleep sessions, diaper changes, and developmental milestones.
4. **Acoustic Audio Clips**: Short audio recordings (8 to 30 seconds) captured when you use the AI Cry Analysis feature.
5. **Vital Signs Telemetry**: Oxygen saturation (SpO2), heart rate (BPM), and perfusion index streams captured during Bluetooth pulse oximeter monitoring sessions.

---

## How Your Data is Stored and Protected

- **Private Encrypted Storage**: All baby photographs and audio recordings are stored in private cloud storage vaults. They are never published publicly or indexed by search engines.
- **Strict Access Isolation**: Your baby's records are isolated to your family. Other MumtaAI users, community members, and healthcare experts cannot view or search your baby's profile or health data.
- **Controlled Caregiver Permissions**: You decide exactly what invited family members or nannies can see by assigning Full, Read-Only, or Limited permissions.
- **Encrypted Transmission**: All communication between your device and MumtaAI is encrypted using modern industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS).

---

## Payment and Credit Card Security

- **Powered by Stripe**: MumtaAI does **not** store or have access to your full credit card number, expiration date, or CVV code.
- **PCI-DSS Compliance**: All billing transactions, renewals, and card updates are securely managed directly through Stripe, a certified PCI Service Provider Level 1 (the most stringent level of payment security available).

---

## Account Security Best Practices for Parents

To keep your account secure:
- **Use a Strong, Unique Password**: Choose a password of at least 8 characters with a mixture of letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid reusing passwords from other websites.
- **Keep Your Email Secure**: Because password reset links are sent to your email, ensure your email account itself is secured with two-factor authentication (2FA).
- **Be Mindful of Caregiver Invites**: Only invite trusted family members or caregivers to your baby's profile, and select the appropriate permission level (such as Read-Only or Limited for casual babysitters).
- **Log Out on Shared Devices**: If you access MumtaAI from a public computer or family tablet, remember to sign out when you are finished.

---

## Your Privacy Rights and Data Deletion

You own your family's data. You have full control over what is kept and what is removed:

### 1. Deleting Individual Records
- You can delete any activity log, developmental milestone, or audio recording directly from your timeline at any time. Deleting an item removes it permanently.

### 2. Deleting a Baby Profile
- As the Primary Parent, you can deactivate or delete a baby profile from the baby settings menu. Once deleted, the profile is immediately removed from your dashboard and all caregiver accounts.

### 3. Complete Account Deletion
- If you decide to stop using MumtaAI, you have the right to request a complete account deletion.
- When your account is deleted, your personal profile, baby profiles, activity histories, audio recordings, and photos are permanently purged from our active systems.
- To request account deletion, navigate to **Settings > Account > Delete Account** or email **privacy@mumtaai.com**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are my baby's cry recordings used for public advertising?
No. Your audio recordings are strictly confidential and are used only to provide you with acoustic cry analysis and personal history tracking. They are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.

### Can healthcare experts on MumtaAI see my baby's records?
No. Healthcare experts only have access to public community discussion threads and articles they author. They cannot view your baby's personal profile, daily logs, or vitals unless you explicitly invite them as a personal caregiver.

### Can a caregiver export or take my baby's data?
Only caregivers granted **Full Access** can download pediatrician summary reports. Read-Only and Limited caregivers do not have permission to export reports.
