# Glossary of Terms — MumtaAI

## Overview

This glossary defines common terms used throughout the MumtaAI application, baby health monitoring, and family caregiving.

---

### Access Level
The specific permission tier assigned to an invited caregiver by the Primary Parent:
- **Full Access**: Can log activities, record cry audio, pair Bluetooth oximeters, view health insights, and update medical notes.
- **Read-Only**: Can view live vitals and past logs, but cannot edit records or record audio.
- **Limited Access**: Can only log routine daily tasks (feeding and diaper changes).

### Activity Timeline
The real-time, chronological log of your baby's daily events—including feedings (breast, bottle, solids), sleep sessions, diaper changes, medications, and developmental milestones.

### Baby Profile
The central digital profile for your child in MumtaAI, storing their name, birth date, growth measurements (weight and height), blood type, medical notes, photos, and personalized vital alert thresholds.

### Caregiver
A partner, co-parent, grandparent, babysitter, or nanny invited by the Primary Parent to collaborate on an infant's profile.

### Cry Analysis
The AI-assisted feature that analyzes an 8-second audio clip of your crying baby to identify the most likely underlying distress reason (e.g., Hunger, Sleepiness, Discomfort, Pain, Colic) and provide actionable soothing tips.

### Cry Categories
The 7 primary reasons for infant distress identified by MumtaAI:
- **Hunger**: Rhythmic, demanding cry signaling the need to feed.
- **Sleepy / Tired**: Whiny, nasal cry indicating fatigue or overtiredness.
- **Discomfort**: Fussing caused by room temperature, restrictive clothing, or awkward positioning.
- **Pain**: Sudden, high-pitched screech followed by a brief breath pause.
- **Diaper Change**: Irritation from a wet or soiled diaper.
- **Colic**: Prolonged, intense bouts of inconsolable crying with abdominal tension.
- **Overstimulated**: Distress caused by too much noise, light, or handling.

### Confidence Rating
The percentage match (e.g., 85%) indicating how clearly the acoustic markers of your baby's cry aligned with a specific distress category.

### Healthcare Expert
A certified pediatrician, neonatal nurse, lactation consultant, or infant sleep coach whose credentials have been verified by the MumtaAI team to publish articles and answer community questions.

### Pediatrician Summary Report
A downloadable PDF or CSV document (available on Plus and Pro plans) that aggregates growth charts, crying patterns, feeding volumes, sleep durations, and vital signs trends to share with your pediatrician during clinical checkups.

### Perfusion Index (PI)
A numeric indicator (typically between 0.2% and 20%) that reflects arterial pulse strength at the pulse oximeter sensor site. A higher number indicates a strong, clear pulse signal.

### Primary Parent
The user who created the infant profile in MumtaAI. Holds exclusive administrative rights to invite caregivers, configure vital alert thresholds, and deactivate the profile.

### Pulse Oximeter
A small non-invasive device placed on your baby's foot, toe, or hand that wirelessly transmits blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and heart rate (BPM) via Bluetooth to the MumtaAI app.

### Pulse Rate (BPM)
Your baby's heart rate measured in beats per minute. Normal resting infant heart rates typically range between 80 and 160 BPM.

### Quiet Hours
A user-defined bedtime schedule (e.g., 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM) that mutes non-critical notifications while allowing urgent vital sign alarms to sound.

### SpO2 (Oxygen Saturation)
The percentage of oxygen carried in your baby's blood. Normal healthy infant readings typically sit between 95% and 100%.

### Stripe Customer Portal
A secure, self-service billing management page powered by Stripe where parents can update their credit card, view and download PDF invoice receipts, or cancel their subscription.

### Sustained Breach
A vital reading (low SpO2 or abnormal pulse rate) that remains outside safe thresholds for **at least 5 continuous seconds** before an audible alarm sounds. This eliminates false alarms caused by temporary kicking or sensor shifting.

### Urgency Meter
A situational indicator (*Low*, *Moderate*, *High*, *Critical*) displayed alongside cry analysis results to help parents gauge how promptly their baby needs comforting or checking.

### Verified Expert Badge
A green checkmark badge displayed next to the name of a medical professional whose license and credentials have been verified by MumtaAI.
