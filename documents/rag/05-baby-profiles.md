# Baby Profiles — MumtaAI

## What is a Baby Profile?

A **Baby Profile** is the central record for your child in MumtaAI. All daily activity logs, acoustic cry recordings, pulse oximeter monitoring sessions, developmental milestones, and caregiver permissions are linked to your baby's profile.

You can create distinct profiles for each of your children, allowing you to track their unique health metrics, schedules, and growth independently.

---

## What Information Can You Store in a Baby Profile?

- **Baby's Name**: Full or preferred name.
- **Date of Birth**: Used by MumtaAI to automatically calculate your baby's age in weeks, months, and days.
- **Gender**: Boy, Girl, or Prefer not to say.
- **Birth Measurements**:
  - Birth Weight (in kilograms)
  - Birth Height / Length (in centimeters)
- **Blood Type**: Optional reference (e.g., A+, O-, B+).
- **Profile Photo**: An avatar photo of your baby displayed on their profile card and dashboard header.
- **Medical & Pediatric Notes**: Freeform notes for allergies, pediatrician contact information, dietary concerns, or special care instructions.
- **Custom Vital Alert Thresholds**: Personalized pulse and oxygen limits tailored to your baby's age.

## Plan Limits on Baby Profiles

The number of baby profiles you can manage depends on your active MumtaAI subscription plan:

| Subscription Plan | Allowed Baby Profiles |
|---|:---:|
| **Free Plan** | **1 baby profile** |
| **Plus Plan** ($9.99/mo) | **Up to 3 baby profiles** |
| **Pro Plan** ($19.99/mo) | **Unlimited baby profiles** (soft cap: 10) |

If you reach your plan's baby limit and wish to add another child (such as for twins or a new baby), you can easily upgrade to Plus or Pro from your **Settings > Subscription** page.

## How to Add a New Baby Profile

1. Log in to your MumtaAI dashboard.
2. Click on your active baby's name or avatar in the top navigation bar to open the baby selector.
3. Click **Add Baby** (or navigate to **Babies > Add New Baby**).
4. Fill in your baby's details:
   - Full Name
   - Birth Date
   - Gender
   - Birth Weight and Height
   - Optional profile photo
5. Click **Save Baby Profile**.
6. The new profile is created immediately and set as your active profile.

---

## How to Switch Between Baby Profiles

If you have multiple children registered:
1. Click the baby selector dropdown located in the top navigation header.
2. Select the baby you wish to view.
3. The dashboard, activity timelines, vitals charts, and cry history will instantly switch to display information for that specific child.

---

## How to Edit a Baby Profile

As your baby grows, you can update their details and medical notes at any time:

1. Go to **Babies** or click **Baby Settings** on the active profile card.
2. Click **Edit Profile**.
3. Update any required fields (such as updated medical notes, weight, or a new photo).
4. Click **Save Changes**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Who can edit baby profiles?**
> Only the **Primary Parent** and caregivers granted **Full Access** have permission to update baby details. Read-Only and Limited caregivers cannot edit baby profiles.

## Customizing Baby Vital Sign Alert Thresholds

Newborns and older infants often have different normal resting heart rate ranges. MumtaAI allows the Primary Parent to customize oximeter alert thresholds for each baby:

1. Navigate to the baby's profile settings and select **Oximeter Alert Thresholds**.
2. Configure safe ranges:
   - **Minimum SpO2 (Oxygen Saturation)**: Default is 90% (allowed range: 50%–100%).
   - **Minimum Pulse Rate (BPM)**: Default is 80 BPM (allowed range: 30–250 BPM).
   - **Maximum Pulse Rate (BPM)**: Default is 160 BPM (allowed range: 30–250 BPM).
3. Click **Save Thresholds**.

When an oximeter is connected, MumtaAI will monitor these thresholds. If your baby's vitals stay outside these safe boundaries for **more than 5 continuous seconds**, an alert will sound and appear on your screen.

## How to Remove or Deactivate a Baby Profile

If you need to remove a profile:
1. Navigate to the baby's profile settings.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Deactivate Baby Profile**.
3. Confirm the deletion prompt.
4. The baby will be removed from your active dashboard and caregiver views.

> [!WARNING]
> Only the **Primary Parent** who created the baby profile has permission to deactivate or delete it. Caregivers cannot delete baby profiles.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I share my baby's profile with my partner or nanny?
Yes! If you are on the Plus or Pro plan, you can invite caregivers under **Family & Caregivers** and give them Full, Read-Only, or Limited access to your baby's profile.

### If I upgrade my plan, do my existing baby logs get transferred?
Yes. Upgrading your subscription does not change your baby data. All existing logs, recordings, and milestone photos remain intact.

### What should I put in Medical Notes?
You can record your pediatrician's name and clinic phone number, known allergies, immunization dates, or specific dietary recommendations.
