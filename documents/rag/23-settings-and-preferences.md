# Settings and Preferences — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI allows you to customize your experience to fit your family's needs. Through the Settings menu, you can manage your personal profile, set your local timezone, tailor notification channels and quiet hours, calibrate vital sign alert thresholds for each baby, and manage your billing.

---

## How to Access Settings

1. Log in to your MumtaAI dashboard.
2. Click your profile avatar or initials in the upper right-hand corner of the navigation bar.
3. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu.

---

## 1. Profile and Account Settings

Under the **Profile** tab, you can manage your personal information:

- **Display Name**: Update your name as shown to invited caregivers and on community discussion posts.
- **Profile Picture**: Upload a personal photo or avatar (supports JPG, PNG, WebP up to 2 MB).
- **Phone Number**: Add an optional contact number for SMS safety alerts.
- **Timezone**: Set your local geographic timezone (e.g., *America/New_York*, *Europe/London*, *Asia/Karachi*).
  > [!TIP]
  > Setting your correct timezone is essential so that timestamps on feedings, sleep logs, and monthly usage counters match your local clock accurately!
- **Language Preference**: Choose your preferred interface language.

---

## 2. Notification Preferences

Under the **Notifications** tab, you can choose which alerts you receive and how they reach you:

### Notification Categories
- **Pulse Oximeter Alarms**: High-priority alerts triggered when your baby's vital signs breach safe boundaries for more than 5 continuous seconds.
- **Cry Analysis Alerts**: Notifications when a cry analysis indicates elevated situational distress.
- **Caregiver Updates**: Alerts when a co-parent or nanny logs feeding, sleep, or medication.
- **Community Replies**: Notifications when an expert or parent responds to your forum thread.
- **Subscription & Billing Notices**: Renewal receipts and monthly quota reminders.

### Quiet Hours (Nighttime Sleep Protection)
Avoid being woken up by non-urgent notifications while your baby is sleeping:
- Turn on **Quiet Hours** and choose your sleep window (e.g., 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM).
- **Emergency Bypass**: Keep **"Allow Urgent Alerts during Quiet Hours"** checked so that critical pulse oximeter alarms will still immediately sound if an active monitoring session detects a sustained breach.

### Alert Chimes and Tones
- Test and select the alert chime tone played on your computer or phone when a vital threshold is breached.

---

## 3. Baby Vitals Alert Thresholds

Each child can have independent vital sign safety limits:

1. Go to **Babies** and click on your baby's settings card.
2. Select **Oximeter Alert Thresholds**.
3. Adjust the safe boundaries:
   - **Low Oxygen Saturation (SpO2 %)**: Default is 90% (allowed range: 50% to 100%).
   - **Low Heart Rate (Pulse BPM)**: Default is 80 BPM (allowed range: 30 to 250 BPM).
   - **High Heart Rate (Pulse BPM)**: Default is 160 BPM (allowed range: 30 to 250 BPM).
4. Click **Save Thresholds**.

---

## 4. Subscription & Billing Settings

Under the **Subscription & Billing** tab:
- View your currently active plan (**Free**, **Plus**, or **Pro**).
- Check your real-time monthly usage meters (e.g., *4/7 Cry Analyses used this month*).
- View your next monthly renewal date.
- Click **Manage Billing & Invoices** to securely open the Stripe Customer Portal to update your payment card, download tax invoices, or cancel auto-renewal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can caregivers change my baby's vital alert thresholds?
No. Only the Primary Parent who created the baby profile can modify vital alert thresholds.

### Will changing my timezone retroactively shift past logs?
Your historical logs are safely stored; updating your timezone simply adjusts how times are displayed on your timeline so that past and future logs align with your current local time.
