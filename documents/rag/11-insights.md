# Insights and Analytics — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI's **Insights and Analytics** dashboard brings your baby's daily activities, cry classifications, and vital sign records together into clear, visual trends.

Instead of guessing whether your baby is crying more this week or sleeping longer stretches at night, the Insights section highlights meaningful patterns to help you optimize sleep, feeding schedules, and comfort routines.

---

## What Insights Does MumtaAI Provide?

### 1. Crying Patterns and Peak Distress Hours
- **Dominant Cry Reasons**: See which distress causes are most frequent (e.g., 55% Hunger, 25% Tired, 20% Discomfort).
- **Peak Crying Hours**: An hourly chart showing what time of day your baby cries most often (e.g., the late afternoon "witching hour").
- **Cry Volume Trends**: Compare daily and weekly crying totals to see if soothing strategies are improving comfort over time.

### 2. Sleep & Feeding Regularity
- **Nap & Night Sleep Breakdown**: Track total hours of daytime naps vs nighttime sleep.
- **Wake Counts**: Monitor how often your baby wakes during night sleep sessions.
- **Feeding Intervals & Volume**: Review average times between feedings and daily bottle volume consumed.

### 3. Vital Sign Trends (Oximeter Sessions)
- **Average SpO2 and Heart Rate**: Review historical trends of oxygen levels and pulse rate during sleep monitoring sessions.
- **Alert History**: See if and when any sustained vital sign drops were recorded.

### 4. Smart Health and Parenting Suggestions
Based on your logged data, MumtaAI surfaces personalized recommendations:
- Suggested nap windows based on wake window trends.
- Gentle tips when crying peaks around the same time each evening (e.g., calming baths or darkened rooms before overstimulation sets in).
- Guidance on growth milestones tailored to your baby's age in months.

## Exporting Pediatrician Reports (PDF / CSV)

When visiting your pediatrician for routine well-baby checks or health concerns, you can bring an organized summary of your child's data.

### What is Included in the Export Report?
- Growth metrics (weight and height progression).
- Activity summary (average daily feedings, bottle volumes, wet diaper tally, sleep durations).
- Cry distribution (primary reasons and peak hours).
- Vital signs summary (average SpO2, resting pulse rates, and any recorded alert events).

### How to Download a Report:
1. Navigate to the **Insights** tab in your dashboard.
2. Click **Export Report** in the top-right corner.
3. Select the desired date range (e.g., Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or Custom Date Range).
4. Choose your preferred file format (**PDF Summary** or **CSV Data**).
5. Click **Download Report**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do my insights look empty?
Insights are generated directly from your daily logs, cry recordings, and oximeter sessions. Once you log at least 2 to 3 days of activities, charts and trend cards will automatically populate with meaningful data.

### Can caregivers view the Insights tab?
- **Full Access Caregivers**: Yes, they can view all charts and export reports (if plan allows).
- **Read-Only Caregivers**: Can browse historical trends but cannot export reports.
- **Limited Caregivers**: Cannot view historical insights or cry analytics.

### How does Multi-Baby Comparison work?
On the **Pro Plan**, if you have twins or multiple young children, you can view side-by-side growth and sleep comparisons to easily observe how each child's patterns differ.
