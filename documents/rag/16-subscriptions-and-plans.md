## Detailed Plan Profiles

### 1. Free Plan ($0 / month)
- **Best for**: New parents who want to experience MumtaAI's core tracking and try AI cry analysis.
- **Key Features**:
  - Manage 1 baby profile.
  - 7 AI cry analyses every month (up to 30-second audio clips).
  - 20 activity logs per month (covering the essentials: Feeding, Sleep, and Diaper changes).
  - 7 days of historical insights and basic trend charts.
  - Access to read community blog articles, download resources, and post 1 forum question per week.

### 2. Plus Plan ($9.99 / month)
- **Best for**: Parents who want shared caregiving with a partner or nanny, comprehensive daily logs, and frequent cry analyses.
- **Key Features**:
  - Manage up to 3 baby profiles.
  - 60 AI cry analyses per month (up to 2-minute audio clips).
  - **Unlimited** activity logs across all 8 activity categories (including Medicine, Milestones, Play, and Bath).
  - Invite up to 2 caregivers per baby with customizable access levels.
  - 90 days of health history and full interactive charts.
  - 1 exportable PDF/CSV pediatric summary report per month.
  - 1-year audio recording retention.

### 3. Pro Plan ($19.99 / month)
- **Best for**: Larger families, twins, parents monitoring long-term health patterns, and power users who want complete peace of mind.
- **Key Features**:
  - **Unlimited** baby profiles (soft cap: 10).
  - **Unlimited** AI cry analyses (up to 5-minute audio clips).
  - **Unlimited** daily care activity logs across all 8 categories.
  - **Unlimited** caregivers per baby.
  - **All-time permanent** insights history and audio retention.
  - **Unlimited** pediatric export reports (PDF and CSV).
  - Side-by-side multi-baby comparison charts.
  - Unlimited community forum posts and blog articles.

## How Monthly Limits and Resets Work

- **Usage Cycles**: Usage limits for cry analysis, activity logs, and report exports reset automatically at the beginning of each monthly billing cycle.
- **Checking Your Current Usage**: You can check your remaining monthly allowances at any time by navigating to **Settings > Subscription**.
- **Hitting a Plan Limit**: If you reach your plan limit for the month, MumtaAI will politely display a message explaining the limit and give you the option to upgrade. None of your saved data is ever lost or deleted when you hit a limit.

---

## How to Upgrade Your Plan

1. Log in to your MumtaAI dashboard.
2. Click your avatar and go to **Settings > Subscription** (or click **Upgrade** in the top navigation).
3. Select either **Plus ($9.99/mo)** or **Pro ($19.99/mo)**.
4. You will be securely directed to Stripe Checkout to enter your payment details.
5. Once payment is confirmed, your account is immediately upgraded and all new features and higher quotas unlock instantly!

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes. You can cancel your paid subscription at any time from **Settings > Billing**. You will retain full access to your plan's features until the end of your current paid billing period, after which your account will return to the Free plan.

### If I downgrade to the Free plan, will my baby's history be deleted?
No. Your historical data, photos, and recordings are preserved according to our retention policies. If you have more than 1 baby profile, your primary baby will remain active, and additional profiles will be temporarily archived until you upgrade again.

### Do invited caregivers need to pay for a subscription?
No. Invited caregivers do **not** need a paid subscription to join your baby's profile. As long as the Primary Parent has an active Plus or Pro plan, invited caregivers can collaborate for free.
