# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — MumtaAI

## Overview

Find answers to common questions about using MumtaAI, managing baby profiles, understanding cry analysis, connecting Bluetooth pulse oximeters, inviting caregivers, and managing subscriptions.

---

## 1. General & Account Management

### What is MumtaAI?
MumtaAI is an intelligent infant care platform that supports parents and caregivers through daily childcare routine tracking (feeding, sleep, diaper changes), acoustic cry analysis, and wireless vital signs monitoring via Bluetooth pulse oximeters.

### How do I sign up for an account?
Go to the **Sign Up** page, enter your full name, email, and password (at least 6 characters), and click **Create Account**. Check your inbox for a verification email, click the link to confirm, and follow the short setup wizard to register your first baby.

### How do I reset my password if I forget it?
On the Sign In page, click **Forgot Password?**, enter your registered email address, and click **Send Reset Link**. Open the email link, enter your new password, and click **Save New Password**.

### How do I change my display name, photo, or timezone?
Click your profile avatar in the upper right-hand corner and choose **Settings**. Under the **Profile** tab, you can update your name, photo, phone number, and timezone.

### What should I do if my account says it is suspended?
If your account has been suspended, please reach out to **support@mumtaai.com** or use our Contact form so our team can review your account.

## 2. Baby Profiles & Caregiver Sharing

### How many baby profiles can I track in MumtaAI?
- **Free Plan**: 1 baby profile.
- **Plus Plan ($9.99/mo)**: Up to 3 baby profiles.
- **Pro Plan ($19.99/mo)**: Unlimited baby profiles (soft cap: 10).

### Can I invite my partner, grandparent, or nanny to share baby tracking?
Yes! Family sharing is available on the Plus and Pro plans. Go to **Family & Caregivers** in your dashboard, click **Invite Caregiver**, enter their email, choose a relationship, and pick an access level (**Full Access**, **Read-Only**, or **Limited**).

### Can I invite caregivers on the Free plan?
No. Caregiver collaboration is available on the Plus and Pro plans. If you are on the Free plan, you can upgrade to Plus to invite up to 2 caregivers per baby.

### What is the difference between Full Access, Read-Only, and Limited caregivers?
- **Full Access**: Can log daily activities, record cry audio, connect oximeters, view insights, and update medical notes.
- **Read-Only**: Can view live vitals and past activity logs, but cannot edit records, record audio, or pair devices.
- **Limited**: Can only log routine daily tasks (feeding and diaper changes); cannot view historical cry predictions or deep health trends.

### Can an invited caregiver delete my baby's profile or remove other users?
No. Only the **Primary Parent** who originally created the baby profile can delete the profile, set vital alert thresholds, or manage caregiver invitations.

## 3. Cry Analysis & Audio Recordings

### How does MumtaAI analyze my baby's crying?
When you record an 8-second clip of your baby crying, MumtaAI filters out background noise, examines acoustic patterns (such as pitch, harmonics, rhythm, and pauses), and compares the sound to known infant distress patterns. Within seconds, it displays the most likely cause, a confidence rating, an urgency score, and gentle soothing recommendations.

### What cry categories can MumtaAI recognize?
MumtaAI recognizes 7 primary infant distress categories:
1. **Hunger**: Rhythmic, escalating cry often accompanied by rooting or sucking gestures.
2. **Sleepy / Tired**: Whiny, nasal cry, often paired with yawning or rubbing eyes.
3. **Discomfort**: Whimpering fussiness caused by room temperature, clothing irritation, or awkward posture.
4. **Pain**: Sudden, high-pitched, intense screech followed by a brief breath-holding pause.
5. **Diaper Change**: Fussy writhing caused by a cold, wet, or soiled diaper.
6. **Colic**: Prolonged, intense bouts of crying with a tense tummy and drawn-up legs.
7. **Overstimulated**: Fussy crying when a baby is overwhelmed by bright lights, loud sounds, or too much activity.

### Does MumtaAI diagnose medical illnesses from crying?
**No.** MumtaAI is an acoustic assistance tool designed for everyday parenting guidance. It is **not** a diagnostic medical device and does not detect infections, respiratory disorders, or medical emergencies. Always consult your pediatrician if your baby seems unwell.

### How many cry analyses can I perform each month?
- **Free Plan**: 7 analyses per month (clips up to 30 seconds).
- **Plus Plan**: 60 analyses per month (clips up to 2 minutes).
- **Pro Plan**: Unlimited analyses per month (clips up to 5 minutes).
Your allowance resets automatically each month on your billing date.

### Can I upload an audio file recorded from a baby monitor or phone memo?
Yes! In the **Analyze Cry** section, select the **Upload Audio** tab and upload any MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG, or WebM file.

## 4. Pulse Oximeter & Vital Signs Monitoring

### What pulse oximeter devices are supported?
MumtaAI supports standard Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) pulse oximeters, including the **Creative Medical PC-60F**, **PC-60FW**, and **Wellue** Bluetooth pulse oximeter family.

### Can I connect a Bluetooth oximeter on an iPhone or iPad?
**No.** Apple does not support the Web Bluetooth standard in Safari or any iOS browser. To connect a pulse oximeter, use **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on an Android phone, Windows computer, or Mac. (Note: Once an oximeter is streaming from a supported device, other family members can view live readings on iOS).

### How do smart sustained alerts work?
Babies move frequently, which can cause harmless, 1-second drops in readings. To avoid false alarms, MumtaAI requires a vital breach (e.g., SpO2 < 90%) to continue for **at least 5 continuous seconds** before sounding an alarm. Once alerted, a 60-second cooldown prevents repeated alarm beeping while you comfort your child.

### What is a normal SpO2 (oxygen saturation) for babies, and when is it concerning?
- **Healthy Baseline**: 95% to 100%.
- **Immediate Newborns**: SpO2 is naturally lower in the first minutes to hours after birth as the lungs adapt to breathing room air.
- **When to Seek Medical Attention**: Beyond the immediate newborn period, an SpO2 consistently **below 90%–92%** may indicate a respiratory infection (such as bronchiolitis or pneumonia) or cyanotic heart conditions. If readings remain below 90%–92% or your baby has trouble breathing, seek urgent medical evaluation.

### What is a normal heart rate (pulse) for babies and children?
Normal heart rates vary based on age and whether the child is awake or asleep:
- **Neonate (< 28 days)**: 100–205 BPM (awake) / 90–160 BPM (asleep)
- **Infant (1–12 months)**: 100–190 BPM (awake) / 90–160 BPM (asleep)
- **Toddler (1–2 years)**: 98–140 BPM (awake) / 80–120 BPM (asleep)
- **Preschool (3–5 years)**: 80–120 BPM (awake) / 65–100 BPM (asleep)
- **School-Age (6–11 years)**: 75–118 BPM (awake) / 58–90 BPM (asleep)
- **Adolescent (12–15 years)**: 60–100 BPM (awake) / 50–90 BPM (asleep)
*(Heart rates temporarily increase during crying, feeding, or fever, and reach their lowest points during sound sleep).*

### What is a normal respiratory (breathing) rate for children by age?
- **Infants (< 1 year)**: 30 to 53 breaths per minute
- **Toddlers (1–2 years)**: 22 to 37 breaths per minute
- **Preschoolers (3–5 years)**: 20 to 28 breaths per minute
- **School-Age (6–11 years)**: 18 to 25 breaths per minute
- **Adolescents (12–15 years)**: 12 to 20 breaths per minute
*(Count breaths for a full 60 seconds when your child is quiet. Fast breathing accompanied by chest retractions or flaring nostrils warrants immediate doctor attention).*

### Will MumtaAI call 911 or an ambulance if vitals drop?
**No.** MumtaAI does **not** dispatch emergency services. If your baby appears unresponsive, pale, blue, or is struggling to breathe, call emergency medical services (911 / 112) immediately.

## 5. Subscriptions, Billing & Plans

### What are the subscription prices?
- **Free Plan**: $0.00 / month
- **Plus Plan**: $9.99 / month
- **Pro Plan**: $19.99 / month

### How do I upgrade my subscription?
Go to **Settings > Subscription**, select **Plus** or **Pro**, and complete checkout securely through Stripe. Your expanded limits and features activate immediately.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to **Settings > Billing** and click **Manage Billing & Invoices** to open the Stripe Customer Portal, then click **Cancel Plan**. You will continue to enjoy your paid plan benefits until the end of your prepaid billing period, with no further charges.

### What happens if I reach a plan limit?
MumtaAI will display a polite notification explaining that you have reached your monthly allowance and offer an upgrade option. Your saved data, history, and photos are never deleted or affected.

## 6. Security & Privacy

### Are my baby's photos and audio clips private?
Yes. All infant photos, vital signs streams, and audio recordings are stored in private, encrypted vaults. They are only accessible by you and the caregivers you explicitly invite.

### Does MumtaAI store my credit card details?
No. All billing information is processed and encrypted directly by **Stripe**, a certified PCI Service Provider Level 1. MumtaAI never sees or stores your full credit card number.

### How can I delete my data or account?
You can delete individual activity entries or baby profiles directly in the app. To delete your entire account and purge all associated data, go to **Settings > Account > Delete Account** or contact **privacy@mumtaai.com**.
