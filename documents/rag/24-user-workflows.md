# User Workflows — MumtaAI

## Overview

This guide provides clear, step-by-step instructions for everyday tasks and procedures in MumtaAI. Use these workflows to quickly accomplish goals in the app.

---

## Workflow 1: How to Sign Up for an Account

1. Go to the **Sign Up** page.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and a secure password (at least 6 characters).
3. Click **Create Account**.
4. Check your email inbox for a verification link and click **Verify Email**.
5. Select your role intent: **"I'm a Parent / Family Member"** (or choose Healthcare Professional if applicable).
6. Complete the baby onboarding wizard by entering your baby's name, birth date, gender, and birth measurements.
7. Click **Complete Setup** to access your dashboard.

---

## Workflow 2: How to Reset Your Password

1. On the Sign In screen, click **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Click **Send Reset Link**.
4. Check your email inbox for the reset link from MumtaAI.
5. Click the link in the email to open the password reset screen.
6. Enter and confirm your new password.
7. Click **Save New Password**, then sign in with your new credentials.

---

## Workflow 3: How to Add an Additional Baby Profile

1. Log in to your MumtaAI dashboard.
2. Click the baby selector dropdown in the top navigation bar.
3. Click **+ Add Baby** (or go to **Babies > Add Baby**).
   > *Note*: The Free plan supports 1 baby profile. If you need to add more children, upgrade to **Plus** (up to 3 babies) or **Pro** (unlimited babies).
4. Enter the new baby's name, birth date, gender, birth weight (kg), and birth height (cm).
5. Optionally upload a profile photo.
6. Click **Save Baby Profile**.
7. Your new baby profile is immediately selectable from the top navigation dropdown.

---

## Workflow 4: How to Invite a Caregiver

1. Verify that your account is on the **Plus Plan** (up to 2 caregivers per baby) or **Pro Plan** (unlimited caregivers). *Caregiver invites are not available on the Free plan.*
2. Select your baby and navigate to **Family & Caregivers**.
3. Click **Invite Caregiver**.
4. Enter the caregiver's email address and select their relation (e.g., Partner, Grandparent, Nanny, Babysitter).
5. Choose their access permission:
   - **Full Access**: Can log activities, record cry audio, connect oximeters, and view full history.
   - **Read Only**: Can view live vitals and activity logs without editing.
   - **Limited**: Can only log routine daily tasks (feeding and diaper changes).
6. Click **Send Invitation**.
7. The caregiver will receive an email invitation, or you can copy the displayed link and send it to them directly.
8. Once the caregiver clicks the link and signs in, they are immediately granted access to your baby's profile.

---

## Workflow 5: How to Record and Analyze a Cry

1. In your dashboard, open the **Analyze Cry** section (or click the microphone icon).
2. Ensure your device's microphone is allowed in your browser settings.
3. Hold your phone, tablet, or laptop roughly 1 to 2 meters (3 to 6 feet) away from your crying baby.
4. Tap **Start Recording**.
5. Let the app record an 8-second sample of active crying.
6. The app automatically cleans background noise, analyzes the sound characteristics, and presents your results within seconds:
   - **Primary Cry Cause** (e.g., Hunger, Sleepy, Discomfort, Colic, Pain).
   - **Confidence Percentage**.
   - **Urgency Meter Rating** (Low, Moderate, High, or Critical).
   - **Soothing Suggestions & Guidance**.
7. Tap **Feedback** at the bottom to let the app know if the prediction was accurate.

## Workflow 6: How to Connect a Bluetooth Pulse Oximeter

1. Turn **ON** your compatible Bluetooth pulse oximeter (e.g., Creative Medical PC-60F, PC-60FW, or Wellue model).
2. Ensure you are using **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on an Android phone, Windows PC, or Mac. *(Note: Direct Bluetooth pairing is not supported on iPhone/iPad due to Apple iOS restrictions).*
3. Gently place the sensor probe on your baby's foot, toe, or hand.
4. Navigate to the **Oximeter** tab in MumtaAI.
5. Click **Connect Oximeter**.
6. Select your oximeter name from the browser's Bluetooth pairing popup and click **Pair**.
7. The connection status will turn green, and your baby's live SpO2 percentage, pulse rate (BPM), and real-time trend graphs will begin streaming.

---

## Workflow 7: How to Log a Daily Activity (Feeding, Sleep, Diaper, etc.)

1. On your dashboard, click **+ Log Activity**.
2. Select the activity type (Feeding, Sleep, Diaper, Medicine, Milestone, Play, or Bath).
3. Fill in the relevant fields:
   - **Feeding**: Breastfeeding sides, bottle amount in ml, or solid food type.
   - **Sleep**: Start and end times (duration calculates automatically) and sleep quality.
   - **Diaper**: Wet, dirty, both, or dry.
   - **Medicine**: Drug name, dosage, and time given.
4. Add any optional notes for your caregivers.
5. Click **Save Activity**.
6. The entry immediately appears on your baby's daily timeline.

---

## Workflow 8: How to Export a Pediatrician Summary Report

1. Ensure you have an active **Plus** or **Pro** plan.
2. Navigate to the **Insights** tab.
3. Click **Export Report** in the upper-right corner.
4. Choose the date range (e.g., Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or a custom range).
5. Select your preferred file format: **PDF Summary** or **CSV Data**.
6. Click **Download**.
7. Save the report to your device or print it to take to your pediatrician appointment.

---

## Workflow 9: How to Upgrade Your Subscription

1. Go to **Settings > Subscription** (or click any **Upgrade** button in the app).
2. Select the plan that fits your needs:
   - **Plus Plan**: $9.99 / month (3 babies, 60 cry analyses, 2 caregivers, 90-day history, report exports).
   - **Pro Plan**: $19.99 / month (Unlimited babies, unlimited cry analyses, unlimited caregivers, all-time history).
3. Click **Upgrade Now**.
4. You will be directed to the secure Stripe Checkout page to enter your card details and any promotional coupon code.
5. Click **Subscribe**.
6. Your account is immediately upgraded with all expanded limits active.

---

## Workflow 10: How to Cancel Your Paid Subscription

1. In your MumtaAI dashboard, go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Manage Billing & Invoices** to open the secure Stripe Customer Portal.
3. Click **Cancel Plan**.
4. Confirm your cancellation.
5. Your plan will remain active until the end of your prepaid monthly billing period, after which it safely transitions to the Free plan. No further charges will occur.
