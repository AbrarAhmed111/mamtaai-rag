# Billing and Payments — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI partners with **Stripe**, the industry-leading secure payment platform, to process all subscription payments, renewals, and invoice receipts. Your credit card information is encrypted and handled directly by Stripe; MumtaAI never sees or stores your full credit card number.

---

## Accepted Payment Methods

Through Stripe, MumtaAI accepts:
- Major Credit & Debit Cards (**Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB**)
- **Apple Pay** and **Google Pay** (available on supported devices and browsers)
- Prepaid cards enabled for recurring online purchases

All transactions are billed in **US Dollars ($ USD)**.

---

## How to Upgrade Your Subscription

Upgrading unlocks higher cry analysis limits, extra baby profiles, caregiver collaboration, and exportable pediatrician reports immediately:

1. Log in to your MumtaAI dashboard.
2. Click your profile icon in the top navigation and select **Settings > Subscription** (or click any **Upgrade** button).
3. Choose the plan that fits your family:
   - **Plus Plan**: $9.99 / month
   - **Pro Plan**: $19.99 / month
4. Click **Upgrade Now**.
5. You will be redirected to the secure Stripe Checkout page:
   - Enter your card number, expiration date, CVC, and billing ZIP code.
   - If you have a promotional discount code, click **Add promotion code**, enter your coupon, and click **Apply**.
6. Click **Subscribe**.
7. Once payment completes, you are returned to your MumtaAI dashboard with your new plan instantly active!

## Managing Your Billing and Invoices (Stripe Customer Portal)

MumtaAI gives you direct self-service control over your payment methods and receipts through the secure Stripe Customer Portal:

### How to Access the Billing Portal:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing** in your dashboard.
2. Click **Manage Billing & Invoices**.
3. You will be securely directed to your dedicated Stripe Customer Portal.

### What You Can Do in the Billing Portal:
- **Update Payment Method**: Add a new credit card or set a default payment method.
- **Update Billing Information**: Edit your billing name, address, or email for receipts.
- **View Payment History & Invoices**: View all past charges and download official PDF receipts/invoices (useful for HSA/FSA reimbursement or personal expense tracking).
- **Cancel Your Subscription**: Stop automatic renewal at any time.

---

## How to Cancel Your Subscription

You can cancel your paid MumtaAI subscription at any time with zero hassle or cancellation fees:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Manage Billing & Invoices** to open the Stripe Portal.
3. Click **Cancel Plan**.
4. Confirm the cancellation.

### What happens when you cancel?
- **No Immediate Interruption**: You retain full access to all your Plus or Pro features until the end of your current paid billing period.
- **No Further Charges**: Your credit card will not be charged again.
- **Smooth Transition**: At the end of the paid period, your account automatically transitions to the Free plan. None of your historical logs or photos are deleted.

---

## Failed Payments & Grace Period

If your card expires or a monthly renewal charge fails:
1. Stripe will automatically notify you by email with a secure link to update your payment information.
2. MumtaAI provides a short grace period during which your account remains active while Stripe attempts to reprocess the charge.
3. If the payment method is not updated after multiple retries, the account will be safely moved to the Free plan until you choose to resubscribe.

## Refunds and Billing Support

If you were charged by accident or experienced an issue with your subscription:
- Please contact our support team at **support@mumtaai.com** or through our in-app **Contact Support** form.
- Include the email address associated with your MumtaAI account and the date of the charge.
- Our team will review your request and issue eligible refunds promptly.
