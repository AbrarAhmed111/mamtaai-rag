# Product Overview — MumtaAI

## What is MumtaAI?

MumtaAI is an intelligent infant care web application designed to support parents and caregivers through daily childcare routines, acoustic cry interpretation, vital signs monitoring, and healthcare expert guidance.

The platform provides a centralized, easy-to-use digital hub where families can:
- Understand infant distress with AI-assisted cry analysis.
- Connect supported Bluetooth pulse oximeters to track oxygen saturation (SpO2) and pulse rate in real time.
- Log daily activities such as feedings, sleep sessions, diaper changes, and developmental milestones.
- Seamlessly share caregiving responsibilities with family members, nannies, or partners.
- Access educational resources, verified expert articles, and community discussions.

---

## Who is MumtaAI For?

MumtaAI is built for three primary groups of users:

1. **Primary Parents**: Mothers, fathers, and legal guardians who set up infant profiles, track daily care routines, interpret cries, monitor vitals, and invite extended family members.
2. **Caregivers**: Partners, grandparents, babysitters, and trusted relatives invited to join a baby's profile with customizable access permissions (Full, Read-Only, or Limited).
3. **Healthcare Experts**: Verified pediatricians, lactation consultants, pediatric nurses, and sleep specialists who share trusted parenting advice, author educational guides, and answer community questions.

---

## Core Problems MumtaAI Solves

- **Understanding Infant Cries**: Distinguishes between common infant distress triggers—including hunger, tiredness, pain, discomfort, diaper needs, colic, and overstimulation—to help parents respond quickly with soothing recommendations.
- **Shared Caregiving Communication**: Replaces fragmented notebooks and messaging threads with a synchronized activity log so all caregivers stay on the same page.
- **Home Vitals Awareness**: Enables non-invasive vital sign monitoring via compatible Bluetooth pulse oximeters, alerting parents to sustained threshold drops.
- **Reliable Guidance**: Gives parents access to curated pediatric articles and verified expert insights, reducing reliance on unvetted internet searches.

## Core Feature Overview

### 1. Baby Profile Management
- Create dedicated profiles for each baby (up to plan limits: Free = 1, Plus = 3, Pro = unlimited).
- Record birth date, birth weight, birth height, blood type, medical notes, and photos.
- Automatic age calculation in months and days.
- Switch seamlessly between multiple baby profiles from the main navigation.

### 2. Family & Caregiver Collaboration
- Invite partners, grandparents, or babysitters via email or secure invitation links.
- Assign granular permissions:
  - **Full Access**: Can log activities, record cry audio, pair oximeters, and view histories.
  - **Read Only**: Can view live vitals and history, but cannot modify records or record audio.
  - **Limited Access**: Restricted to logging specific daily routines (e.g., bottle feeding and diaper changes).
- Revoke caregiver access at any time.

### 3. Daily Activity Tracking
- Log 8 daily care activities:
  - **Feeding**: Breastfeeding (left, right, or both breasts), bottle feeding (formula or expressed milk in ml), and solid foods.
  - **Sleep**: Track start/end times, wake counts, and sleep quality.
  - **Diaper Changes**: Wet, dirty, both, or dry.
  - **Medication**: Track medication name, dosage, and time given.
  - **Milestones**: Record developmental milestones (motor, social, cognitive) with photos.
  - **Tummy Time, Play & Bath**: Keep track of daily routines and durations.
- Filter and search the activity timeline by date and activity type.

### 4. Acoustic Cry Analysis
- Record infant crying directly through your web browser or upload an audio file.
- The system analyzes the acoustic characteristics of the cry in seconds.
- Receive the most likely cry reason (e.g., Hunger, Sleepy, Discomfort, Colic), a confidence rating, situational urgency level, and soothing tips.
- Flag potential medical red flags with prompts to seek medical attention if necessary.
- Submit feedback on predictions to help personalize and improve future guidance.

### 5. Bluetooth Pulse Oximeter Monitoring
- Wirelessly connect compatible Bluetooth Low Energy pulse oximeters (Creative Medical PC-60F, PC-60FW, and Wellue models).
- View continuous live vitals: Oxygen Saturation (SpO2 percentage), Pulse Rate (beats per minute / BPM), and Perfusion Index (PI).
- Live interactive charts and session history.
- Customizable alert thresholds (with sustained 5-second breach detection to avoid false alarms).

### 6. Analytics & Insights
- Daily, weekly, and monthly activity breakdowns.
- Crying pattern charts and peak crying hour identification.
- Automated care suggestions based on logging trends.
- Downloadable health summary reports (CSV/PDF) to share with your pediatrician (available on Plus and Pro plans).

### 7. Community & Expert Resources
- **Resource Library**: Downloadable feeding charts, sleep routines, and developmental checklists.
- **Editorial Guides**: Articles written by verified healthcare professionals and experienced parents.
- **Discussion Forums**: Ask parenting questions, exchange tips, and read answers from verified experts.

## Important Safety Disclaimers & Limitations

> [!CAUTION]
> **MumtaAI is NOT a Medical Diagnostic Tool or Life-Support Monitor**
> 
> 1. **Informational Use Only**: MumtaAI provides parental support and acoustic classification based on sound patterns. It does **not** diagnose medical conditions, illnesses, or congenital disorders.
> 2. **Not a Medical Device or Clinical Monitor**: The pulse oximeter feature is designed for parental awareness and logging. It is **not** a replacement for hospital-grade monitors or clinical medical supervision.
> 3. **No Emergency Services Dispatch**: MumtaAI does **not** call emergency services (such as 911, 112, or local ambulances). If your baby shows signs of medical distress, difficulty breathing, cyanosis (blue skin/lips), lethargy, or high fever, contact your doctor or local emergency services immediately.
> 4. **No Medical Prescriptions**: Healthcare experts participating in the community do not prescribe medications or establish formal doctor-patient relationships through the platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is MumtaAI free to use?
Yes. MumtaAI offers a Free plan that allows you to manage 1 baby profile, log up to 20 activities per month, and perform 7 cry analyses each month. For larger families, extended limits, caregiver collaboration, and exportable reports, Plus and Pro plans are available.

### Do I need special hardware to use MumtaAI?
No. All core features—including baby tracking, cry analysis, community discussions, and insights—work on any smartphone, tablet, or computer. An external Bluetooth pulse oximeter is only needed if you want to use the optional live vitals monitoring feature.

### Can both parents access the same baby's information?
Yes. On the Plus and Pro plans, you can invite your partner, nanny, or family members as caregivers so everyone shares real-time access to the baby's timeline and health logs.
