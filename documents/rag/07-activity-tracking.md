# Activity Tracking — MumtaAI

## Overview

MumtaAI's Activity Tracking feature helps parents and caregivers keep a shared, accurate timeline of their baby's daily care routines, feeding schedules, sleep sessions, medical doses, and growth milestones.

Whether you are tracking how much milk your baby drank, monitoring wet diapers, or recording when fever medication was given, every entry is saved to your baby's interactive timeline in real time.

---

## Supported Activity Types

MumtaAI supports 8 distinct daily childcare activity types:

### 1. Feeding Log
Track all nutrition, feeding methods, and volumes:
- **Breastfeeding**: Select left breast, right breast, or both, and record start and end times.
- **Bottle Feeding**: Log expressed breast milk or formula volume (in milliliters / ml).
- **Solid Foods**: Record the type of purees, cereals, or finger foods offered and your baby's response.

### 2. Sleep Tracking
Monitor sleep patterns, nap lengths, and nighttime awakenings:
- **Start and End Times**: MumtaAI automatically computes total sleep duration in hours and minutes.
- **Sleep Quality**: Note whether sleep was deep, light, restless, or interrupted.
- **Wake Count**: Record how many times your baby woke up during the session.

### 3. Diaper Changes
Maintain regularity and track hydration:
- **Diaper Condition**: Wet, Dirty, Both (Wet + Dirty), or Dry.
- **Notes**: Add optional observations on color, texture, or diaper rash concerns.

### 4. Medicine Administrations
Keep an accurate record of vitamins and pediatric medications:
- **Medicine Name**: Record the medication (e.g., Infant Paracetamol, Vitamin D drops).
- **Dosage**: Specify the exact amount administered (e.g., 2.5 ml, 1 drop).
- **Time Given**: Exact timestamp of administration to prevent accidental double-dosing.

### 5. Developmental Milestones
Celebrate and remember key growth achievements:
- **Category**: Motor skills (rolling over, crawling), Cognitive, Social (first smile, laughing), or Language (babbling, first words).
- **Description**: Add a personal note about the moment.
- **Photo Memories**: Attach photos to commemorate the achievement.

### 6. Play & Tummy Time
Log awake-time stimulation, sensory play, and daily tummy time sessions to track physical development.

### 7. Bath Routines
Record bath times, hygiene routines, or skincare treatments.

### 8. Other Custom Notes
Log special events such as doctor visits, vaccinations, or outings.

## How to Log a Daily Activity

1. Log in to your MumtaAI dashboard and make sure your baby is selected.
2. Click the **+ Log Activity** button (or choose an activity icon such as Feeding, Sleep, or Diaper).
3. Select the activity category.
4. Fill in the relevant details (e.g., amount in ml, start/end time, or diaper type).
5. Add any helpful notes for your partner or caregiver.
6. Click **Save Activity**.
7. The activity immediately appears at the top of your baby's daily timeline.

---

## How to View and Filter the Activity Timeline

1. From the dashboard, navigate to **Activity Timeline**.
2. **Filter by Date**: Select a specific day or view the weekly timeline.
3. **Filter by Category**: Toggle filters to view only feedings, sleep sessions, or diaper changes.
4. Review total daily counts (e.g., total ounces/ml consumed today, total daytime sleep, and wet diaper tally).

---

## How to Edit or Delete an Activity Log

If you made a typo or logged an event at the wrong time:
1. Locate the entry on your **Activity Timeline**.
2. Click the **Edit** (pencil) or **Delete** (trash) icon on that activity card.
3. Update the details and click **Save Changes**, or confirm deletion.

> [!NOTE]
> Primary Parents and caregivers with **Full Access** can edit and delete activities. Caregivers with **Limited Access** can log entries but cannot modify existing history.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I forget to log a feeding when it happened?
You can log an activity retrospectively at any time! Simply adjust the time and date in the activity form to match when the feeding or sleep actually took place.

### Can my partner and I log activities at the same time?
Yes. On the Plus and Pro plans, both parents and invited caregivers can log activities from their respective phones or computers, and the shared timeline updates in real time.

### How do I export my baby's activity logs for the pediatrician?
On the Plus and Pro plans, visit the **Insights** section and click **Export Report** to download a clean PDF or CSV summary of your baby's feeding, sleep, and diaper trends.
